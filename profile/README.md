### What Pathbreak does

Pathbreak maps an AWS account, works out which identity and exposure chains reach sensitive resources, and reports the single change that cuts off the most risk. It validates the paths end to end and hands back a short, ordered list of what to fix with the command that breaks each one.

- **Validated attack paths.** IAM privilege escalation, cross-account trust, and exposed credentials or public entry points, confirmed without running exploits.
- **Ranked by blast radius.** The paths that reach the most are at the top, each with the change that removes them.
- **Built for real estates.** 44 compliance frameworks, inventory, integrations (Jira, Security Hub, S3), SSO, and bring your own model.
